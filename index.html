01 · RigRoute · Live

The GPS that knows you're driving a truck.

Truck-safe routes around low bridges, tight turns and no-truck streets, free in your phone's browser.

  • Routes sized to your rig: height, weight, length and axles
  • Shared live hazards, with drivers voting still-there or gone
  • Hours-of-service helper (informational, not a certified ELD)
  • Ask RigRoute, an AI helper for road questions

For: truck drivers, owner-operators and small fleets. No account, no download, $0.

Day one (Nov 4, 2026, our analytics): 41 devices · 48 questions asked

Start driving free →
Full breakdown: RigRoute

Problem

Consumer GPS routes a 53-foot rig like a sedan: into low bridges, tight turns and no-truck streets. Truck tools are often paid, account-walled or bundled with heavy ELD suites.

Who it's for

Truck drivers, owner-operators and small fleets, starting in NYC and the Northeast.

How it makes money

Free for drivers. Planned: optional fleet/dispatcher pro tools (pricing not set).

How it works

  • Truck profile (height, weight, length, axles) shapes every route; NYC truck routes first
  • Look-ahead danger checks against OpenStreetMap height, weight and hazmat limits, with spoken warnings and reroute
  • Shared guest hazards with still-there/gone votes; hours-of-service helper (not an ELD)
  • Ask RigRoute AI help, capped at 20 questions per device per day; works in the phone browser, no account

Live today

Live at ace9x.github.io/truckers-gps. Guest-only, free. 41 unique devices and 48 Ask RigRoute questions on the first day of analytics (Nov 4, 2026).

Next (planned, no dates promised)

  • Optional pro tools for fleets and dispatchers once driver density justifies it
  • Self-hosted routing and map data for fleet-scale reliability
  • Metro-by-metro growth with opt-in area stats

03 · Car Service app · White-label

Your base's own branded app.

Rider app, driver app and dispatch console under your base's name and colors, with upfront fares and real human dispatch.

  • Rider app: GPS pickup, upfront locked fare, live car on the map, ride PIN, receipts and ratings
  • Driver app: ride offers with fare and $/mile before accepting, turn-by-turn navigation, airport queues, earnings
  • Dispatch console: live driver map, trip queue, one-tap assign and phone bookings
  • Your fares and rules: flat airport rates, no-surge option, English and Spanish, installs from the browser

For: car service and livery bases that want their own app instead of a marketplace. Launch client: Rocapulco Supreme, Far Rockaway, Queens.

Full breakdown: Car Service app (white-label)

Local car service and livery bases compete with big ride-hail apps but have no app of their own, so riders call or drift to marketplaces with surprise fares.

Who it's for

Car service and livery bases that want their own branded app. Launch client: Rocapulco Supreme, Far Rockaway, Queens.

How it makes money

License the app to bases. Pricing not set yet.

How it works
